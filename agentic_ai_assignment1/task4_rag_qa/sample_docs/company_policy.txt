Acme Corp Employee Handbook (Sample Excerpt)

1. Refund Policy
Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase, provided the
product is unused and in its original packaging. Refund requests after 30
days but within 90 days are eligible for store credit only. No refunds are
issued after 90 days from the date of purchase. Digital products are
non-refundable once downloaded.

2. Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Employees must be available on core hours (10 AM - 4 PM local time) regardless
of work location. Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are
reviewed every 6 months.

3. Leave Policy
Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of paid time off per month, capped at 24
days per year. Unused leave up to 5 days may be carried over to the next
calendar year. Sick leave is tracked separately and does not count against PTO.

4. Data Security Policy
All employees must enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) on company
accounts. Laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled. Any suspected
security incident must be reported to the security team within 1 hour of
discovery via the #security-incidents Slack channel or security@acmecorp.com.

5. Expense Reimbursement
Employees must submit expense reports within 30 days of incurring the
expense. Reports submitted after 30 days require director approval. Meals
during business travel are reimbursed up to $75/day domestically and
$100/day internationally.
